## Descripción breve (máx. 80)
Learn your first English words with pictures and sounds. Fun and simple!

## Descripción completa
Happy Words is a simple and colourful game to learn your first English words.

• 20 easy words: animals, food and everyday things
• See the picture, hear the word and pick the right answer
• Tap the picture to listen again
• Earn stars and play again and again
• No ads, no purchases, no internet needed
• No data collected – safe for kids

Perfect for young children starting to learn English.
