Happy Words – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Happy Words is a simple English vocabulary game for children.

Data we collect

None. Happy Words does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal or non-personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and does not access the internet.

Word pronunciation uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device; all processing happens locally.

Children

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. No data from children is collected.

Contact

If you have any questions, contact the developer through the email shown on the app's Google Play page.

Política de privacidad (Español)

Happy Words no recoge, almacena ni comparte ningún dato personal. No tiene cuentas, anuncios, analíticas, compras ni acceso a internet. La pronunciación usa el motor de voz del propio dispositivo, sin enviar nada fuera.
